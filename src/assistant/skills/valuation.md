This is a starter template, not a fact. It ships switched off. Duplicate it, replace everything
below with your own figures and conventions, and switch it on — then the assistant will read it when
a question depends on something the app cannot derive from statement files.

Delete any line that does not apply to you.

## Assets Ledger does not hold

    Flat, 12 Rue Example      — €320,000, my estimate, Jan 2026
    Car                       — €8,500, trade-in value, Mar 2026
    Pension (employer scheme) — €41,000 as of the last annual letter, Dec 2025

State a date with every figure. The assistant will repeat it back rather than presenting your
estimate as a current fact.

## Debts Ledger does not hold

    Mortgage — €214,000 outstanding, Feb 2026, 1.4% fixed to 2031
    Family loan — €3,000, no interest

## Conventions

Things you want interpreted a particular way, for example:

    Revolut top-ups are transfers, not income.
    My tax year runs April to March.
    "Groceries" includes the weekly market stall, which shows up as a cash withdrawal.
    The joint account is shared 50/50 — halve anything from it when you talk about my own spending.

## What this cannot do

Nothing written here changes what the app will and will not answer. It cannot make the assistant
project a cash runway, model a scenario, or tell you whether a purchase is a good idea — those are
declined by design, not for want of information.
